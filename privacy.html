Privacy Policy

Work Tracker · Last updated: October 2026

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Work Tracker stores your work sessions, sleep records, walking data, meditation sessions, notes, and reminders locally on your phone. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the encrypted backup feature.

2. Google Sign-In (Optional)

Google Sign-In is completely optional. If you choose to sign in, the app requests these limited scopes:

  • Calendar (read-only): to display your Google Calendar events inside the app. The app never creates, modifies, or deletes calendar events.
  • Drive app data: to store your encrypted backups in your own Google Drive app folder, only if you enable backup.

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party or advertising network.

3. Permissions

The app requests only permissions needed for its features (activity recognition for step counting, notifications for reminders, alarms for scheduling). No data collected through these permissions is sent anywhere.

4. Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data. You may revoke the app's Google access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
