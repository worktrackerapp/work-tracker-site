About the App

Work Tracker is a personal Android app that helps you track your daily activities — work hours, sleep, walking, meditation, phone usage, notes, and reminders — all stored privately on your own device.

💼 Work Time

Log work sessions and track daily totals.

😴 Sleep

Automatic and manual sleep tracking with smart Sleep Day grouping.

🚶 Walking

Step counting, walking time, distance, calories, and achievements.

🧘 Meditation

Timer-based and manual meditation sessions.

📝 Notes & Reminders

Colorful notes and scheduled reminders.

📅 Calendar Sync

Optionally view your Google Calendar events inside the app (read-only).

Privacy First

All your records stay on your phone. Google Sign-In is optional and only used for viewing your calendar events. Read our Privacy Policy.
